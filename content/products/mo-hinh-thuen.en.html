The CALLISTA yacht model captures the look of a modern motor superyacht: a long white hull, stepped decks, tinted glazing and generous open living areas. At 90 cm long it is the longest piece in our cruise ship and yacht models collection and a luxurious focal point for any room.

Superyacht style

“Superyacht” is the general term for large private yachts, usually from about 24 m upwards, run by a professional crew and equipped like a small floating hotel. The name Callista has been carried by several real yachts, including a motor yacht of about 38 m launched in 2000 with five guest cabins. Our model is inspired by this type of vessel: a raised bow, a tiered superstructure that tapers towards the stern, a wide foredeck and a stepped aft deck for relaxing and sunbathing.

Yachts like this balance beauty and comfort: a glass-walled saloon, outdoor dining, a sun deck on the top level, and a radar mast with communication antennas.

Model details

Model 0071 measures 90 × 12 × 25 cm. It is handmade from wood and finished mainly in glossy white:

  • A long white hull with a dark bottom and decks stepping down towards the stern.
  • Bands of tinted windows around the saloon and bridge.
  • A top sun deck with radar mast and antennas.
  • Interior decorative lighting that gives a warm glow in the evening.
  • A sturdy reddish-brown wooden base.

To bring out its luxury feel, CALLISTA is finished with great care: the hull is sanded many times before painting, each deck is built separately and assembled, and the windows are fitted by hand so the interior light glows evenly through them. With the lights on, the model looks like a yacht at anchor on a calm evening, adding a warm accent to the room. It is a favourite choice for clients who want a model that works as both decoration and ambient lighting.
