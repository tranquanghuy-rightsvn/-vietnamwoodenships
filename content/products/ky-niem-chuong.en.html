Inspired by single sculling

The figure shows single sculling – a discipline in which one rower propels a narrow, long boat with two oars. It is one of the oldest and most prestigious events in competitive rowing. At Henley Royal Regatta in England, the men's Diamond Challenge Sculls has been raced since 1844, and its very first prize was a diamond scarf pin. In the United States, the Philadelphia Challenge Cup was once regarded as the world amateur single sculling championship.

Thanks to trophies like these, the image of a lone sculler has become a familiar symbol of endurance and discipline. That is why a commemorative piece with this figure is not only for the sporting world; it is also a fitting corporate gift, where the spirit of rowing – persistence, teamwork and focus on the finish line – is always valued.
