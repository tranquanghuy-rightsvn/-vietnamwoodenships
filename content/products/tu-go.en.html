The Wooden Cabinet NT 002 is a cabinet-and-shelf unit in the form of a wooden boat stood on end, its bow pointing to the ceiling. Inside, the hull is divided into shelves, X-shaped cubbies and a stemware rack, turning the boat into a mini wine bar or a characterful display case. At 2 metres tall and 80 cm wide it takes up very little floor space yet makes a strong statement in a living room, dining room or home bar.

The upright boat shelf: a nautical favourite

Canoe and boat shelves are popular pieces in seaside homes and lakeside cabins in North America and Europe. Many makers build them around 1.5 metres tall or more, combining bookshelves, a cupboard and wine storage within the hull. Their appeal lies in using height instead of width, and in the image of a wooden boat – something that brings back memories of holidays on the water.

Features of the NT 002

  • Size: 80 cm wide, 200 cm high.
  • Hull: curved wooden sides with a crisp gunwale; some versions are painted blue outside with natural wood inside.
  • Display shelves: wooden tiers for bottles, jugs, books, models or souvenirs.
  • Stemware rack: rails beneath a shelf hold wine glasses upside down, tidy and easy to reach.
  • Bottle storage: X-shaped cubbies or drilled racks keep wine bottles lying on their sides.
  • Handmade: planks are fitted, bent and finished by hand with a light sheen that shows the grain.

Use and care

As a wine cabinet, store wine bottles lying down so the cork stays in contact with the wine and does not dry out. Keep the cabinet somewhere cool, out of direct sun and away from the stove or oven – good for the wine and for the wood, which can otherwise fade or shrink. Because it is tall and slim, fix the top to the wall for safety, especially with children or pets at home.

It also works as a bookcase or a display for ship models and collectibles. Dust regularly with a soft cloth and never spray water directly onto the wood. See our user guide for more on caring for wooden pieces.
