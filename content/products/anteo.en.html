ANTEO is a model of a steam tug – the small but powerful workhorse that spent its days in European harbours towing, pushing and nudging much larger ships to and from their berths. With its full, rounded hull, tall funnel and wide aft deck for the towing gear, ANTEO has a sturdy, honest look that is full of character. Vietnam Wooden Ships builds the model by hand in wood, leaving the deck and superstructure in natural wood tones.

The prototype: an Italian harbour tug

Anteo is a well-known harbour tug among ship model enthusiasts. Italian kit makers (the Panart brand of Mantua Model) describe it as representing a type of steam tug built in large numbers and widely used in Italian ports in the years before the Second World War. The same type remained a typical sight in many southern European harbours until as late as the 1970s.

Steam tugs of that era usually had a large boiler amidships, a tall funnel, a small wheelhouse forward and an open aft deck for the towing hook and cable. A low hull, rounded bow and large propeller gave them strong pulling power at low speed – exactly what was needed for manoeuvring big ships in the tight spaces of a port.

Model details

  • Hull: dark blue-black topsides, green underwater body and a fine white line along the hull; bulwarks finished in natural wood.
  • Deck and superstructure: a wooden wheelhouse with windows, red-and-white lifebuoys, a brass-coloured bell on the bow and a windlass forward.
  • Engine area: a tall black and grey funnel, deck boxes and hatches amidships.
  • Stern: towing hook, rudder and propeller clearly modelled below the hull.

Each part is cut, shaped and fitted by hand, and the paint is built up in several coats for a smooth, lasting finish. The natural wood grain on the deck gives the model the warmth of a real wooden vessel.
