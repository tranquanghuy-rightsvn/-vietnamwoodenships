The ASTOR ship model recreates MS Astor, a familiar name in European ocean cruising for more than three decades. At 80 cm long and 38 cm tall, it is a substantial multi-deck model that makes a fine centrepiece in our cruise ship and yacht models collection.

MS Astor – a German-built cruise ship

MS Astor was built by Howaldtswerke-Deutsche Werft in Kiel, West Germany, and sailed her maiden voyage from Hamburg in January 1987. She was originally planned as a combined liner and cruise ship. In 1988 she was sold to the Soviet Union and renamed Fedor Dostoevskiy, while still being chartered to West German tour operators. In 1995 she regained the name Astor, and from 1996 she belonged to the German company Transocean Tours.

The ship was about 176 m long and 22.6 m wide, carrying roughly 550–650 passengers depending on configuration. She was loved by German and British guests for her European cruises and long voyages to Australia. After decades of service she was withdrawn and scrapped around 2020–2021, which makes a model like this a meaningful keepsake.

Model details

Model DL 001 measures 80 × 12 × 38 cm and is handmade from wood, painted in the ship’s livery:

  • A white hull with a blue stripe along the side and red below the waterline.
  • The ASTOR name and logo at the bow, and a tiered superstructure with neat rows of windows.
  • A row of red-orange lifeboats on each side.
  • A radar mast, white radar domes and an aft funnel with the company emblem.
  • A black wooden base with wood-toned supports.

The model is built on a wooden frame, planked to form the hull and sanded many times before painting. Each superstructure deck, window band, railing and lifeboat is made and fitted by hand, so every ASTOR has its own character while keeping the proportions of the real ship. The white paint is applied in several thin coats for a smooth surface that keeps its colour for years. For many of our customers, the model is a way to remember a voyage on a ship that no longer sails.
