The Bar Counter NT 005 is a bar and service counter built in the shape of a boat: a planked wooden hull with a glossy lacquer, a bow that sweeps forward and a natural wood top running the length of the hull. In the reference photos it serves as the checkout and reception desk of a nautical-themed clothing store, standing out against wide-plank floors and a boarded ceiling. It is a piece of furniture that tells a story to every customer who walks in.

Boat counters in commercial interiors

The counter is where guests linger longest in a bar, restaurant or shop, so many designers make it the focal point of the brand. A hull shape is especially fitting for brands linked to the sea, travel, water sports or seafood: the soft curve of the sides, the bow that leads the eye and the warmth of timber. Compared with a plain box counter, a boat counter gives a space depth and personality.

Features of the NT 005

  • Hull: planked in the manner of boatbuilding and finished in a high-gloss coat that picks out each seam; the bow can be painted in a contrast colour or carry a logo.
  • Counter top: light natural wood that follows the curve of the hull, wide enough for a till, screens and paperwork.
  • Staff side: shelves and storage can be built in to suit your needs.
  • Handmade: the hull curves and planking are shaped by hand; dimensions are agreed for each project.

Sizes and layout

Common bar design guides put a bar top at about 102–107 cm (40–42 in) high, paired with stools whose seats sit around 75–76 cm (30 in) from the floor, while checkout and reception counters are usually lower, closer to kitchen-counter height of about 86–91 cm. An overhang of 20–30 cm on the guest side gives knee room. When ordering, share your floor plan and intended use so the workshop can advise on dimensions.

Wipe the top with a well-wrung damp cloth, use coasters and don't let water or spirits stand on the wood. More care tips are in our user guide.
