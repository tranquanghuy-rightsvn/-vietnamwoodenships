The Wooden Table NT 003 is a coffee table shaped like a small wooden rowing boat, topped with a clear glass panel cut to follow the curve of the bow. Looking down through the glass you see the whole interior of the hull – the thwarts, the keel line and the planked sides – as if a little boat had just been pulled up the beach and set down in the middle of the living room. It is a piece for people who love the sea, love handmade woodwork and want a living room with a real talking point.

The boat coffee table: a much-loved nautical classic

Turning a hull into a coffee table is not a new idea. Antique and design marketplaces regularly feature tables made from rowing boats, racing sculls and dories set beneath glass, and pieces that keep the structure of a real boat can command high prices. What they share is the glass top: it lets you see inside the hull – the seats, the frames, the run of the planks – so the table is both a useful surface and a display piece.

Nautical style suits many homes: seaside apartments, modern houses in white and blue, or classic interiors full of dark timber. A single boat-shaped table is often enough to set the theme for a whole room without piling on accessories.

What makes the NT 003 special

  • Size: 140 cm long, 50 cm wide and 40 cm high – the familiar height of a coffee table in front of a sofa.
  • Hull: handmade with evenly curved sides, a pointed bow and a rounded stern, with cross seats inside just like a real rowing boat.
  • Finish: a warm mid-to-dark brown with a soft sheen that brings out the grain.
  • Glass top: a clear teardrop-shaped panel that follows the outline of the gunwale and gives a flat surface for cups, books or decor.
  • Base: X-shaped wooden legs that cradle the hull firmly, much like a boat resting on stands in a boatyard.

Placement and care

The table is at its best in front of a sofa, in a hotel lobby, a café or an office lounge. Try placing a few small objects inside the hull – a compass, shells, a miniature oar or books about the sea – to add to the story; the glass keeps them dust-free.

Clean the glass with a soft cloth and ordinary glass cleaner, and wipe the wood with a dry or barely damp cloth. Avoid spots in full sun all day or directly under an air-conditioning vent, as wood can fade or shrink. More care tips are in our user guide.
