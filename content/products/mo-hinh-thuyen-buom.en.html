The sloop rig – simple and easy to learn

The boat is rigged as a sloop: one mast, with a mainsail behind it and a jib in front. Small-boat sailing guides describe this as the most common arrangement on dinghies and daysailers, because a single mast and two sails are easy to learn and control – many sailing schools use sloops for beginners. The jib may be small, but it helps the air flow more efficiently over the mainsail, so the boat sails noticeably better when heading towards the wind.

Small wooden sailing boats also have a charm of their own: they are quiet, gentle, close to nature and have a classic beauty that synthetic boats rarely match. With the breezes of Vietnam's coasts and river regions, a wooden sailboat is a delightful way to enjoy outings, photography and holiday experiences.
