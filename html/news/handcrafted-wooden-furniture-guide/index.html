Handcrafted wooden furniture has a character all its own: every table and cabinet carries a grain pattern that never repeats, the marks of plane and chisel still show the craftsman's hand, and the longer you live with it, the richer and warmer the wood becomes — something mass-produced furniture rarely achieves. Alongside our model ships, Vietnam Wooden Ships also makes wooden furniture and decorative pieces. This guide helps you choose the right piece for each room, pair it with nautical-themed decor and care for your wooden furniture so it stays beautiful for years.

Why choose handcrafted wooden furniture?

Mass-produced furniture is usually optimized to be fast, cheap and uniform. Handcrafted wooden furniture, by contrast, is made one piece at a time, so the craftsman can select each board, work each joint and adjust details to match the design. The difference shows in several ways:

  • One of a kind: the grain and color of every piece are unique. Two tables of the same design still have their own "faces".
  • Finish in the details: edges, corners and joints are worked by hand, allowing careful work in places machines struggle to reach.
  • Repairable: solid wood can be sanded, polished or refinished when the surface gets scratched, extending its life by many years.
  • Lasting beauty: wooden furniture does not go out of style as quickly as many other materials, and over time the wood takes on a deeper, more attractive tone.

The same hands that build model ships — work that demands millimeter precision — bring real advantages to furniture making: smooth curves, tight joints and a carefully finished surface. You can see every design in our wooden furniture category.

How to choose handcrafted wooden furniture for each room

Measure before you choose

The most common mistake when buying wooden furniture is choosing on impulse, only to find the piece is too large or too small for the room. Before ordering, measure the length and width of the area, the ceiling height (for tall cabinets) and, above all, the doors, stairways and elevators the piece has to pass through. For a large piece such as the 3-meter boat-shaped table, this step is essential.

Leave room to use it comfortably

  • Around dining and meeting tables: leave enough space to pull out chairs and walk behind seated people.
  • In front of cabinets: leave enough room to open the doors fully without hitting the bed or a table.
  • Coffee tables: place them within easy reach of the sofa without getting in the way of your legs.

Choose to match the overall style

Wood suits almost any style, but each style calls for a different approach. In a modern room with light walls, a large wooden piece such as the boat-shaped table becomes a warm focal point against a minimalist backdrop. In classic or Indochine-style interiors, you can combine several wooden pieces and add decor such as boat paintings, sailing ship models and warm lighting for a nostalgic atmosphere.

Caring for handcrafted wooden furniture

Wood is a natural material that "breathes" with the humidity around it: it swells when it absorbs moisture and shrinks as it dries. Understanding this helps you care for your furniture properly.

Where to place it

  • Avoid long periods of direct sunlight, which fades the finish and dries out the wood, making it prone to cracking.
  • Do not place furniture right next to a heat source or directly in the airflow of an air conditioner.
  • Avoid damp spots and walls that let moisture through; leave a small gap between the furniture and the wall so air can circulate.

Everyday cleaning

  • Dust with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth, wiping along the grain.
  • Do not use harsh cleaners, alcohol-based solutions or floor cleaner on wooden surfaces.
  • Wipe up water, coffee or wine spills on tables and bar counters immediately.
  • Use coasters and trivets to prevent water rings and heat marks.

Periodic care

After some time in use, the surface may look dull or pick up small scratches. You can use a polish made specifically for wooden furniture, testing it first on a hidden spot. For deep scratches or heavily damaged surfaces, have a craftsman sand and refinish the piece — one of the great advantages of solid wood over many other materials. Frequently asked questions

Does handcrafted wooden furniture warp?

Wood is a natural material, so it always moves with humidity. Keeping furniture in a dry, well-ventilated place away from strong sun and heat sources, and cleaning it properly, keeps warping and cracking to a minimum.

What space suits the 300 × 120 cm boat-shaped table?

Given its size, the boat-shaped table suits a meeting room, a spacious dining room or a reception lobby. Measure the room and the delivery route before ordering.

What wood is the dressing table made of?

The 80 cm dressing table is made of melaleuca wood. For other designs, please contact us for specific advice.

Can I order custom dimensions?

You are welcome to contact us to discuss the dimensions and design you have in mind; the workshop will advise on what is feasible for each request.

How should I clean a wooden tabletop?

Use a soft cloth, dry or slightly damp, wipe along the grain, avoid harsh cleaners and wipe spills dry immediately.
