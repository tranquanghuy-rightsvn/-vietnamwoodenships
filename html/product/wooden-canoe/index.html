About this product

The CANOE (in Vietnamese, xuồng) is a real wooden boat, 4.5 metres long and paddled by hand, made for gentle trips on rivers, canals, lakes and calm waters. Its long, slim hull has a raised bow and stern, is built from many narrow wooden planks and finished in varnish to keep the natural beauty of the wood. It carries the spirit of Vietnam's river culture, with a clean finish that suits both recreation and decoration.

From the Mekong Delta xuồng to a leisure boat

In the Mekong Delta, small wooden boats have for generations been the main way people travel, trade and earn a living. The best-known is the xuồng ba lá, or “three-plank boat”, named after its original construction: one wide plank forms the bottom and two more form the sides. Delta boatmen paddle them with a single oar, standing up, or even with their feet. Traditional boatbuilding still survives in craft villages such as Long Hậu commune in Đồng Tháp province.

Today, as more and more of these boats are made of composite materials, a handmade wooden canoe has a cultural and aesthetic value of its own. The Vietnam Wooden Ships CANOE keeps the traditional shape, while its hull is built from many narrow planks for smoother curves and extra strength.

Boat details

  • Product code: TB0011.
  • Dimensions: 450 cm long, 120 cm wide, 45 cm hull depth.
  • Hull: narrow planks running the length of the boat, raised bow and stern, and a wooden rail around the gunwale.
  • Interior: thwart seats, a storage compartment and a flat wooden floor.
  • Finish: golden varnish that brings out the grain and helps the surface resist water.
  • Propulsion: a single paddle, as shown in the photos taken on the river.

The canoe is built entirely by hand. Since it is a working boat meant for real water, please discuss the number of people, the waters you plan to use and the safety equipment you need with the workshop, so we can advise you properly.

Use ideas

This wooden canoe is ideal for eco-tourism sites, countryside homestays and riverside or lakeside resorts where guests love to paddle and enjoy the scenery. It also makes an unusual decorative piece: placed in a garden, used as a flower display, or as a feature for a restaurant with a Mekong theme. On the water, always wear a life jacket and choose calm conditions.
