About this product

The L’AUSTRAL model portrays the luxury expedition cruise ship L’Austral, operated by the French line Ponant. At 70 cm long but only 9 cm wide and 24 cm tall, the model is slim, low and yacht-like, quite different from the bulk of a multi-deck mass-market liner. It is one of the most elegant pieces in our cruise ship and yacht models range.

L’Austral – Ponant’s expedition ship

L’Austral was built by Fincantieri at Ancona, Italy, and entered service in 2011. She is about 142 m long, with roughly 132 cabins and suites for around 260 guests. She belongs to a series of near-identical Ponant ships, alongside Le Boréal and Le Soléal, designed in a “mega-yacht” spirit: much smaller than typical cruise ships, but more intimate and more flexible.

With a hull strengthened for ice, L’Austral regularly sails expedition voyages to Antarctica and the Arctic, as well as Mediterranean and Asian itineraries. Ponant’s signature livery is a dark blue-grey hull, a crisp white superstructure and two slim black funnels aft.

Model details

Model DL 003 measures 70 × 9 × 24 cm. It is handmade from wood and painted in the real ship’s colours. Details include:

  • A dark blue hull with red boot-topping and the name L’AUSTRAL on the side.
  • A multi-deck white superstructure with rows of cabin balconies.
  • A forward radar mast, two slender black funnels and orange lifeboats on both sides.
  • An aft deck with a pool and wood-toned terraces.
  • A black-painted wooden base with two supports.

The hardest part of building L’AUSTRAL is keeping the long, slim profile while still showing every deck. Our craftsmen balance the proportions of each superstructure level, the curve of the bow and the sloping stern so the model never looks heavy. Balconies, windows and railings are made separately and fitted to the hull, then the model receives several thin coats of paint so the dark blue and white stay crisp and clearly separated. The result is a model that reads as a small luxury yacht rather than a bulky liner, exactly like the real ship.

Display and gift ideas

Thanks to its narrow 9 cm beam, L’AUSTRAL fits on slim shelves, sideboards, console tables and desks. It is a fine keepsake for anyone who has sailed on an expedition cruise, for professionals in travel and tourism, or for lovers of clean, modern design.
