About this product

This Dressing Table is a bedroom set of table, mirror and stool, distinguished by a mirror frame carved with flowing leaves and scrolls in a classic style. It is offered in several woods – melaleuca (tràm), pine and gõ – in 80 cm or 1 m lengths, for anyone who wants a practical vanity corner with the elegance of traditional carved furniture.

Three woods, three characters

Melaleuca (tràm) is mostly harvested from plantation forests in Vietnam. It ranges from golden to reddish brown and is hard and solid once properly kiln-dried and treated – a good balance of durability and cost, and a more sustainable choice than natural-forest timber. Pine is light, pale and boldly grained, suiting rustic or modern bedrooms. Gõ is a prized Vietnamese hardwood: heavy, hard, red-brown with handsome grain that deepens with age – the choice for furniture meant to last for many years.

Whatever the wood, furniture in Vietnam's hot, humid climate must be dried and treated against insects and warping before it is made up. It is worth asking about this when you order.

Design details

  • Carved mirror frame: an arched crest with layered leaves and scrolls – the most labour-intensive part of the set.
  • Side shelves: small curved tiers either side of the mirror for perfume, cosmetics and jewellery.
  • Drawer and cabinet: fronts carved in relief, on gently shaped legs.
  • Matching stool: a square seat on curved legs in the same tone.
  • Finish: a glossy honey-brown coat that brings out the grain and wipes clean.

Placement and care

Place the dressing table near natural light – ideally falling from in front of or beside the mirror – but out of direct sun on the wood and glass all day. Keep it away from the bathroom door, where steam can dampen the wood. Dust the carving with a soft brush, then wipe with a dry cloth, and stand cosmetics and perfume on a tray so solvents don't mark the finish. See our user guide for more care advice.

Because every carved frame is worked by hand, the pattern of leaves and scrolls differs slightly from one set to the next, and the colour of the wood will mellow gradually over the years. Many owners find that this slow change is exactly what makes solid carved furniture feel more personal with time.
