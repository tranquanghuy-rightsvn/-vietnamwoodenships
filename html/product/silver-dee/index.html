About this product

The Silver Dee model is a miniature modern motor yacht in the style of boats built for long cruises with family and friends. A smart navy hull, white multi-level cabins, warm wooden decks and a blue canopy over the upper helm give it a fresh, refined look – quite different from the old-world charm of our tall ships.

The modern flybridge motor yacht

Silver Dee belongs to the family of flybridge motor yachts – boats with an open upper helm station – which are among the most popular cruising yachts today. These boats have broad, stable hulls and plenty of living space: a saloon and sleeping cabins inside, an aft deck for relaxing, and an airy covered flybridge from which to steer and enjoy the view. They are typically used for extended coastal cruising, fishing trips and holidays on the water.

We could not find reliable records of a specific full-size vessel named Silver Dee, so this description focuses on the yacht style and the real details of the model.

About the model

This model, code DT017, measures about 60 × 18 × 30 cm and is handmade in wood at Vietnam Wooden Ships. Details you can see on the piece:

  • A navy hull with white trim, a dark red bottom and a band of windows along the side.
  • White cabins with large windows and bright metal railings around the decks.
  • A flybridge with a blue fabric canopy, mast, antenna and radar.
  • Planked wooden decks above and a swim platform with ladder at the stern.
  • Orange lifebuoys, a small tender on deck and many fine fittings.
  • A reddish-brown wooden base with cradles holding the hull securely.

What makes a modern yacht model convincing is precision: straight sheer lines, crisp paintwork and small fittings that look right at scale. On Silver Dee the craftsmen shape the hull and superstructure in wood, paint each colour band by hand and then add the railings, canopy, lifebuoys and other details one at a time. The result is a clean, polished model that suits contemporary homes and offices just as well as a classic tall ship suits a traditional study.

Display suggestions

At 60 cm long and 30 cm high, Silver Dee suits a desk, TV console, display cabinet in a modern living room or an office. Its blue-and-white palette brings a fresh seaside feel to modern, minimalist interiors. It makes a meaningful gift for yacht owners, sea anglers, business people, marine tourism companies and resorts.
