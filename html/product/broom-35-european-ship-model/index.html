About this product

The BROOM 35 EUROPEAN model recreates a classic British motor cruiser of the kind used for leisure trips on rivers, canals and coastal waters. At 60 cm long, 16 cm wide and 27 cm tall, it is compact yet full of detail, and the easiest piece to place in our cruise ship and yacht models collection.

The Broom 35 European – a British family cruiser

Broom is a long-established boatbuilder in Brundall, Norfolk, in the heart of the famous Norfolk Broads waterways. The Broom 35 European was built in the 1970s: a motor cruiser of about 10.6–10.7 m (35 ft) with a GRP hull, typically fitted with twin diesel engines and berths for around six people, plus toilets and a shower.

With roomy cabins, a moderate draught and an apartment-like layout, the Broom 35 European served many European families on long holidays along rivers and canals. Plenty of these boats are still cared for and change hands today, a sign of how well they were built.

Model details

Model DL0057 measures 60 × 16 × 27 cm. It is handmade from wood and painted in a classic cruiser style:

  • A white hull with navy stripes along the side and a dark bottom.
  • A helm area with windscreen, a blue canopy and railings around the deck.
  • Rows of cabin windows, a door and small details such as lights and a flagstaff.
  • A reddish-brown wooden base with two brass supports.

The appeal of this model lies in its honesty and warmth. Unlike a flashy superyacht, the BROOM 35 EUROPEAN evokes slow, happy days on the water with family. The hull is made from wood, carefully sanded and painted in several thin coats; the navy stripes are drawn by hand and the windows and railings are fitted piece by piece, so the model keeps its good looks on display for many years. Its modest size also makes it easy to move between rooms or take to a new office.

Display and gift ideas

Thanks to its compact size, the model suits desks, bookshelves, glass cabinets and living room corners.
